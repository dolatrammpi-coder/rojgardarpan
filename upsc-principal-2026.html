Educational Qualification & Experience

Post Essential Qualification
Principal
  • Master’s Degree from a recognized University/Institute.
  • Bachelor of Education (B.Ed.) from a recognized University/Institute.
  • 10 years’ teaching experience as Vice Principal / Post Graduate Teacher / Trained Graduate Teacher in a recognized High School / Higher Secondary School / Senior Secondary School / Intermediate College.

Desirable: Doctorate Degree and experience of administrative charge of a recognized school/college.

Vice Principal
  • Master’s Degree from a recognized University/Institute.
  • Bachelor of Education (B.Ed.) from a recognized University.
  • Either 2 years’ experience as Post Graduate Teacher OR 3 years’ experience as Trained Graduate Teacher.

Selection Process

  • Combined Recruitment Test (CRT)
  • Shortlisting
  • Interview / Personality Test
  • Final Merit

Final Merit Weightage: CRT : Interview = 75 : 25

Recruitment Test के बाद shortlisted candidates को Interview के लिए बुलाया जाएगा।

Combined Recruitment Test (CRT) Exam Pattern & Syllabus

Exam Detail Information
Duration 2 Hours
Maximum Marks 300 Marks
Question Type Objective Type / Multiple Choice Questions
Medium English & Hindi
Negative Marking प्रत्येक गलत उत्तर पर उस प्रश्न के निर्धारित अंकों का 1/3 कटेगा।

Syllabus Topics
  • General Knowledge including Contemporary Social, Economic and Cultural Issues
  • Hindi and English Language Skills

  • Management and Financial Administration
  • Office Procedure
  • Pedagogy in Education
