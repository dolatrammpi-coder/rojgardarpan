AIIMS NORCET 11 Age Limit & Eligibility

  • Age Limit: 18 to 30 Years.
  • Age will be calculated with reference to the closing date of online registration.
  • Age relaxation is available to eligible reserved/special categories as per applicable Government/AIIMS rules.
  • SC/ST: 5 Years relaxation.
  • OBC: 3 Years relaxation.
  • PwBD: 10 Years, subject to the conditions mentioned in the official notification.
Educational Qualification:
  • B.Sc. (Hons.) Nursing / B.Sc. Nursing from an Indian Nursing Council/State Nursing Council recognized Institute or University.
  • B.Sc. (Post-Certificate) / Post-Basic B.Sc. Nursing from a recognized Institute/University.
  • Candidate must be registered as Nurse & Midwife with State / Indian Nursing Council.
  • OR Diploma in General Nursing Midwifery (GNM) from a recognized Institute/Board/Council.
  • GNM candidates must be registered as Nurse & Midwife with State / Indian Nursing Council.
  • GNM route candidates must have 2 years' experience in a minimum 50-bedded hospital after acquiring the required educational qualification, as applicable to participating AIIMS.

AIIMS NORCET 11 Selection Process

  • Stage-I: NORCET Preliminary Examination
  • Stage-I qualifying examination है और final merit/rank के लिए इसका उपयोग नहीं किया जाता।
  • Stage-I qualify करने वाले candidates को notified scheme के अनुसार Stage-II के लिए बुलाया जाएगा।
  • Stage-II: NORCET Main Examination
  • Final merit मुख्य रूप से Stage-II NORCET Main के performance के आधार पर तैयार की जाएगी।
  • Qualified और eligible candidates से Institute/Hospital choice & preference मांगी जाएगी।
  • Seat allocation merit, eligibility, choices/preferences और reported vacancies के आधार पर होगा।
  • इसके बाद respective Institute/Hospital द्वारा document verification तथा अन्य applicable formalities होंगी।

AIIMS NORCET 11 Exam Pattern

Stage Details
Stage-I
NORCET Preliminary
  • Mode: Online CBT
  • Duration: 90 Minutes
  • Total Questions: 100 MCQs
  • Total Marks: 100
  • General Knowledge & Aptitude: 20 Questions
  • Nursing syllabus: 80 Questions
  • Negative Marking: 1/3 mark for each wrong answer
  • 5 sections, each containing 20 questions
  • Each section gets 18 minutes
Stage-II
NORCET Main
  • Mode: Online CBT
  • Duration: 180 Minutes
  • Total Questions: 160 MCQs
  • Total Marks: 160
  • Questions are based on the Nursing course syllabus at the essential qualification level.
  • Case-scenario based questions are included for testing nursing skills competency.
  • Negative Marking: 1/3 mark for each wrong answer
  • 4 sections, each containing 40 questions
  • Each section gets 45 minutes

  • PwBD candidates को additional 5 percentile relaxation as applicable.
  • UR / EWS: 50%

AIIMS NORCET 11 Syllabus

NORCET-11 का syllabus उम्मीदवार की essential educational qualification और applicable experience के अनुसार होगा।

Stage-I में Nursing course syllabus के साथ General Knowledge और Aptitude के questions शामिल हैं।

Stage-II में essential qualification level पर पढ़ाए जाने वाले Nursing courses के पूरे syllabus से questions पूछे जाएंगे, जिसमें case-scenario based nursing competency questions भी शामिल हैं।

Detailed syllabus अलग notification में नहीं दिया गया है; official notification में syllabus को qualification/experience के अनुसार बताया गया है।
